# Spaced repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals fights the forgetting curve. Each
successful recall pushes the next review further out.

The forgetting curve, first described by Ebbinghaus, shows memory decays
exponentially unless it is reinforced. Retrieval is the reinforcement.

Active recall — trying to retrieve an answer before checking it — is far more
effective than re-reading. The effort of retrieval is what strengthens memory.

Spaced repetition software schedules reviews just before you would forget,
which is the most efficient point to reinforce a memory.
