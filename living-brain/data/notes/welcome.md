# Welcome to your living brain

This is a sample note. Anything you drop into `data/notes` as a `.md` or `.txt`
file becomes part of your second brain.

The living brain ingests your notes, splits them into chunks, and embeds each
chunk into a vector. At night it consolidates everything into a digest that
surfaces recurring themes and unexpected connections between ideas.

## How to use it

- Write freely. Notes can be meeting minutes, reading highlights, ideas, or
  journal entries.
- Run `make ingest` whenever you add notes, or just let the nightly loop do it.
- Ask questions with `make search Q="what did I decide about vector databases"`.

The goal is a knowledge base that gets more useful the more you feed it.
