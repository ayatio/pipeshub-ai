# Note-taking systems

A Zettelkasten is a web of atomic notes linked by association rather than
organized into folders. Value emerges from the connections between notes.

The idea of a "second brain" is to offload memory to an external, searchable
store so your mind is free to think rather than to remember.

Atomic notes — one idea per note — are easier to link and recombine. Large
documents hide their ideas; small notes surface them.

Links between notes are where insight lives. Two notes you wrote months apart
can, once connected, suggest something neither said alone.
